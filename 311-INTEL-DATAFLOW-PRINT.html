Forecasts & Events

  • Base44 demand predictions and venue calendars

  • Vehicle Clustering: Cross-vendor pileup detection (~20m radius)
  • Complaint Mapping: Geographic matching + operator attribution
  • Hotspot Analysis: YoY & WoW trend comparisons by zone
  • Policy Violation: Boundary crossing detection (25m, 50m, 100m)
  • Forecast Integration: Demand signals for dispatch planning
